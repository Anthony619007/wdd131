Ghana's cocoa

Ghana, formerly known as the Gold Coast, is Africa's largest gold producer. The country produces over 4.8 million ounces of gold annually, making it one of the world's top gold producers.

  • 🏆 Largest gold producer in Africa
  • 💰 Gold accounts for 30% of exports
  • ⛏️ Historic Ashanti gold tradition

Ghana's Cocoa Excellence

Ghana is the world's second-largest cocoa producer, known for producing the highest quality cocoa beans used in premium chocolates worldwide.

  • 🍫 World's 2nd largest cocoa producer
  • 🌱 800,000+ farming families
  • ⭐ Premium quality beans
