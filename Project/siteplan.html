🌿 eWiseHome | Reduce E-waste at Home

Site Identity

Domain: ewisehome.org (conceptual)

Why "eWiseHome"? The name blends e-waste with wise decisions and home — emphasizing practical, household-level action. It's memorable, positive, and signals empowerment. The .org domain fits our educational, community-driven mission.

Site Purpose

eWiseHome provides a clear, actionable blueprint for reducing electronic waste at home. The website serves as a go-to resource for individuals who want to minimize their environmental footprint by extending the life of electronics, recycling responsibly, and buying sustainable tech.

  • 📱 What e-waste is and why it's a fast-growing crisis
  • 🛠️ Actionable tips: repair, upgrade, maintain devices
  • ♻️ Local & online recycling/donation directories
  • 🛒 Guidance on repairable, sustainable electronics
  • ✅ Decision tree: Repair, Donate, Recycle, Keep

User Scenarios

“I have 3 old phones and a dead laptop. Where can I recycle them safely without risking my personal data?”

The site provides a searchable map of certified recyclers, data-wiping instructions, and a repair-or-recycle flowchart.

“Which brands offer spare parts, repair guides, and long-term software updates?”

Includes a buyer's guide to repairable electronics with iFixit scores and right-to-repair champions.

“What's the simplest daily habit to reduce e-waste at home?”

Practical micro-tips: extending charge cycles, energy-saving modes, donating to schools, and repair swaps.

Color Schema

#2B6E3C - Primary Green (headings, nav, buttons)
#F4A261 - Warm Accent (CTA, hover, highlights)
#EEF5E8 - Soft Background (page backdrop)
#1E2F2F - Dark Forest (body text, footer)

The green creates a trustworthy, natural identity. Orange draws users to interactive tools like checklists and recycling locators.

Typography

Playfair Display - Headings & Titles

Elegant, bold, perfect for section headers

Roboto - Body Text, Navigation, Paragraphs

Clean, readable, modern sans-serif

Only 2 primary fonts maintain clean, accessible design. Monospace optional for code/references.
