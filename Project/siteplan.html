🌱 eWiseHome

Site / Domain Name: eWiseHome.org (conceptual domain for class)

Why this name? “eWiseHome” combines “e-waste” + “wise choices” + “home.” It’s memorable, positive, and focuses on actionable household habits. The word “Wise” suggests smart consumption, repair culture, and sustainable electronics management — perfectly aligned with helping families reduce e-waste at home. The .org domain fits the educational and community-driven mission.

📌 Site Purpose

The eWiseHome website delivers practical, step-by-step guidance for individuals and families to reduce electronic waste at home. The site will include:

  • Clear definitions of e-waste and its environmental impact.
  • Actionable tips for extending device lifespan (repair, maintenance, upgrades).
  • Local & online recycling/donation resources with interactive guidance.
  • Buyers’ guide for repairable, sustainable electronics.
  • Smart decision flowchart (repair, donate, recycle, keep).
  • Curated directory of take-back programs and certified recyclers.

The primary goal is to empower users to move from confusion to confident action — reducing toxic e-waste while saving money and conserving resources.

🧑‍💻 Scenarios (User Questions)

🔍 Scenario 1

“I have three old smartphones and a broken laptop sitting in a drawer for years. Where can I safely recycle them near me without compromising my personal data? And should I try to repair the laptop first?”

The site answers with a local recycler map, data-wiping checklist, and a decision tree to evaluate repair viability vs. responsible recycling.

♻️ Scenario 2

“I’m about to buy a new tablet, but I want to choose a brand that designs repairable and long-lasting devices. Which brands offer spare parts and repair guides?”

The site provides a sustainability scorecard for popular electronics, highlights right-to-repair champions, and suggests certified refurbished options.

🎨 Color Schema

Primary Green (#2B5E3B) – headings, main buttons, header background, interactive elements.
Accent Orange (#F5A65B) – highlights, call-to-action, hover states, important alerts, logo accent.
Soft Background (#F4F7F2) – body background, soft contrast.
Dark Forest (#1E2F2F) – main body text, footer text, subtle contrast.

Usage notes: Headings and navigation will use #2B5E3B to create a fresh, natural feel. Accent #F5A65B draws attention to key checklists, the decision tree, and “repair/donate” badges. White cards on light green background ensure readability.

✒️ Typography

Primary font (headings & subheadings): 'Work Sans' – bold, modern, highly legible for titles and section headers.

Secondary font (body text, navigation, paragraphs): 'Inter' – clean, neutral, excellent readability on screens, used for all main content, lists, and buttons.

Fallback stack includes system-ui, sans-serif. No more than two primary fonts, maintaining minimalism and accessibility.
