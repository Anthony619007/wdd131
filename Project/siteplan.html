⚡ eWaste Management Site Plan

A professional roadmap for a responsible e-waste reduction platform — combining user education, recycling locators, and circular economy tools.

Site Identity

Domain: ewisecircle.org (conceptual)

Name rationale: "eWiseCircle" merges e-waste wisdom with circular economy — echoing Zoomlion Ghana’s commitment to sustainable waste management. The name emphasizes closing the loop at home.

Core Purpose

eWiseCircle provides an interactive guide to reduce household e-waste through repair tutorials, certified recyclers database, data sanitation tools, and sustainable buying guides. The platform acts as a digital companion for conscious tech users.

  • 📊 Real-time e-waste impact stats
  • 🛠️ Right-to-repair resources & step-by-step guides
  • 🗺️ Interactive recycling hub (Zoomlion-like service map)
  • 🤝 Donation network for functional electronics

User Scenarios

“My laptop broke after 3 years — is it recyclable or repairable? I need local certified centers.”

The site will offer ZIP-based recycling locator (Zoomlion style coverage) and AI-assisted repair-or-recycle decision engine.

“I run a small community workshop — where can I donate 20 old monitors responsibly?”

Dedicated donation hub connecting NGOs, schools, and refurbishers — plus tax-deduction receipts.

“What is the environmental impact of my old smartphone if I keep it in a drawer?”

Carbon impact calculator, material recovery insights, and behavioral nudges to recycle or resell.
