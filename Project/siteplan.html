Why "eWiseHome"? The name blends "e-waste" with "wise decisions" and "home" — emphasizing practical, household-level action. It's easy to remember, positive, and signals empowerment. The .org domain fits our educational, community-driven mission.

📌 Site Purpose

eWiseHome provides a clear, actionable blueprint for reducing electronic waste at home. The website serves as a go-to resource for individuals who want to minimize their environmental footprint by extending the life of electronics, recycling responsibly, and buying sustainable tech.

  • 📱 What e-waste is and why it's a fast-growing environmental crisis
  • 🛠️ Actionable tips: repair, upgrade, maintain devices
  • ♻️ Local & online recycling/donation directories
  • 🛒 Guidance on purchasing repairable, sustainable electronics
  • ✅ Interactive decision tree: "Repair, Donate, Recycle, or Keep?"
  • 🗺️ Curated list of certified e-waste recyclers
Responsible e-waste management matters

🧑‍🌾 Scenarios

🔋
“I have 3 old phones and a dead laptop. Where can I recycle them safely without risking my personal data?”

eWiseHome will answer with a searchable map of certified recyclers, step-by-step data wiping instructions, and a "repair or recycle?" decision flowchart.

💡
“Which brands offer spare parts, repair guides, and long-term software updates?”

The website includes a buyer's guide to repairable electronics, right-to-repair champions, and certified refurbished products.

🏠
“What's the simplest daily habit to reduce e-waste at home?”

Practical micro-tips: extending charge cycles, using energy-saving modes, donating functional electronics, and hosting repair swaps.
